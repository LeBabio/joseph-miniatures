Privacy Policy – Le Protocole Publisher

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Le Protocole Publisher ("the app") is a personal tool used only by the creator of the educational channel Le Protocole to upload their own videos to their own TikTok account.

1. Data we access

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic): open ID, display name and avatar of the connected TikTok account, used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Video upload and publishing (video.upload, video.publish): used only to send the creator's own videos and captions to their TikTok account.

2. Data we store

Only the OAuth access and refresh tokens of the owner's TikTok account, stored privately on the owner's own server, plus the IDs of videos already published. No data about other TikTok users is collected.

3. Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to any third party. Data is only exchanged with TikTok through its official API.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept as long as the integration is used and deleted when it is stopped. Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions).

5. Children

The app has no users other than its owner and collects no data from minors.

6. Contact

Direct message to the Instagram account @le_proto_cole_.
